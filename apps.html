Quick guide

How to install an app on your phone or computer

Pro APEX apps are web apps — Android and desktop only for now, installed straight from the browser, no App Store or Google Play needed.

🤖

On Android (Chrome)

Open the app, tap the menu (⋮) in the top-right corner, then tap "Add to Home screen" or "Install app".

💻

On desktop (Chrome/Edge)

Open the app and look for the install icon (⊕) in the address bar, then click "Install".

Why isn't there an iOS version? These apps are currently built and tested for Android and the web. Get in touch if iOS support would help your team.
