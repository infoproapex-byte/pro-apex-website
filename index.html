Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you work with care providers?

Yes. Pro APEX is available for maintenance, repairs and practical home safety improvements for care providers, supported living environments, landlords and occupied homes.

Can you provide a record of completed work?

For business clients, a simple completion record can be provided, including the work carried out, date and photographs where appropriate.

Do you supply materials?

For private customers, the standard service is labour-only unless otherwise agreed. For business maintenance arrangements, the supply of materials can be discussed as part of the agreed job.

How quickly can you visit?

I usually aim to visit within 48 hours for an initial assessment, subject to availability and the nature of the job.

Do you do gas or F-Gas air-conditioning work?

No. These services are outside my qualifications. Where appropriate, I can recommend suitably registered specialists.

What is the Staff Break Manager?

Staff Break Manager is a simple digital tool designed to help businesses, particularly care environments, keep track of staff breaks in a straightforward way.
